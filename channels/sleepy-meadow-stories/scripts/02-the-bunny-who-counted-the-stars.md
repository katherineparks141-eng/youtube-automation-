# The Bunny Who Counted the Stars
Channel: Sleepy Meadow Stories (original characters — no filming, fully automated)
Format: Narrated illustrated bedtime story | Target length: ~5 min | Audience: toddlers/preschoolers (2-5) + parents
Pillar: Pip the Bunny (2nd story — continues from "The Bunny Who Was Afraid of the Dark")

---

**[0:00–0:15] COLD OPEN / HOOK**
(VO, soft, warm, slow pace — true "bedtime" cadence)

Pip the bunny was tucked in snug, the curtain open just a crack the way he liked it now.
No shadows to worry about tonight. But Pip's eyes would not close. His legs wanted to
wiggle. His ears wanted to listen to every little sound. Pip's body was ready for bed...
but Pip's thoughts were still wide awake.

**[0:15–1:00] SETUP**

"I can't make my thinking go quiet," Pip told Mama Rabbit with a sigh. His thoughts kept
hopping from one thing to the next — the acorn he found, the puddle he jumped, whether
tomorrow would be sunny. Mama smiled and smoothed his ear. "Busy thoughts before bed are
very normal, little one. Even grown-up rabbits get them. Want to try something that
always helps me?"

**[1:00–2:00] THE PROBLEM GROWS**

Pip nodded, even though one of his ears was already drifting toward a new thought about
breakfast. Mama carried him gently to the window. "Oona showed me this trick," she said.
Outside, the meadow was dark and quiet, and the sky was scattered with stars, more than
Pip could ever count — though his wiggly brain certainly wanted to try all at once, which
only made his thoughts spin faster.

**[2:00–3:30] THE TURN**

"Not all at once," Mama laughed softly. "One at a time. Find just one star, and give it a
little hello." Pip found the brightest one first. "Hello, star." He breathed in slow,
and breathed out slower. Then he found a second star, a little dimmer. "Hello, star."
Breathe in. Breathe out. High above, Oona the owl hooted once from her branch — which in
owl means "You're doing it just right." By the fifth star, Pip's legs had stopped
wiggling. By the eighth, his eyes felt heavy as summer honey.

**[3:30–4:30] RESOLUTION**

"The stars don't rush," Mama whispered, "and neither do your thoughts, once you slow down
to meet them one at a time." Pip found one more star — his ninth — and this time he
didn't even finish saying hello before his eyes closed all on their own. His busy
thoughts had quieted down to just one, soft and simple: *goodnight, meadow.*

**[4:30–5:00] GOODNIGHT CLOSE / CTA**
(VO slows further, almost a lullaby cadence)

So if your thoughts are hopping around tonight instead of settling down, try what Pip
tried: find one star — even just a picture of one, even just the idea of one — say a
quiet hello, and breathe. Then find another. The meadow will still be there tomorrow, and
so will your dreams.

Goodnight, little one. Sleep soft, sleep safe. We'll see you in the meadow again soon.

[End screen: soft chime, link to the next Sleepy Meadow story]
